Dataset Description:
This dataset contains synthetic banking, customer, and transaction data designed for financial analysis, fraud detection, and data science projects. It includes customer demographics, bank transaction details, and financial summaries of bank branches. The data incorporates realistic patterns, missing values, and noise, making it suitable for machine learning model training.

Files Included:
1.customer_data.csv

Contains customer demographic and profile details.
2.transaction_data.csv

Contains customer transaction and investment records.
3.bank_data.csv

Contains bank branch revenue and financial performance data.
Columns in Each Dataset:

1.Customer Dataset (synthetic_customer_data.csv)

Customer_ID - Unique customer identifier (Primary Key)
Age - Age of the customer
Customer_Type - Type of customer (Business, Employee, Individual)
City - City of residence
Region - Geographic region

2.Transaction Dataset (synthetic_transaction_data.csv)

Transaction_ID - Unique transaction identifier (Primary Key)
Customer_ID - Customer identifier (Foreign Key)
Account_Type - Type of account (Savings, Current, Business)
Total_Balance - Account balance after transaction
Transaction_Amount - Amount involved in the transaction
Investment_Amount - Amount invested
Investment_Type - Type of investment (Fixed Deposit, Mutual Fund, etc.)
Transaction_Date - Date of transaction

3. Bank Dataset (synthetic_bank_data.csv)
Branch_ID - Unique bank branch identifier (Primary Key)
City - City where the branch is located
Region - Geographic region
Firm_Revenue - Total revenue generated by the branch
Expenses - Total expenses incurred by the branch
Profit_Margin - Profit margin calculated for the branch
